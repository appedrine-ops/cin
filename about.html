Why CIN exists

A place to create, discover and connect.

CIN is built around a simple idea: people should have room to express themselves, discover fresh perspectives and find communities that share their interests.

✦

Our mission

To provide an accessible social space where people can share creativity, express ideas and connect through videos, photos, stories and conversations.

◎

Our vision

To grow into a welcoming digital community that helps creative voices and meaningful connections reach beyond borders.

◇

Our promise

To keep building CIN with creativity, usefulness and community in mind, while being clear about what is available and what is still developing.

The story behind the platform

Built from an idea to create something meaningful.

CIN began with an ambition to build a social platform that gives people a place to share their creativity and connect with others. Its name captures that ambition: Create. Inspire. Network.

Built in Uganda, CIN reflects the belief that useful digital products can be imagined, designed and developed from anywhere. The platform continues to evolve as its creator works to shape the experience and improve what it offers.

The person behind CIN

Meet the founder

Mugonyi Edrine Dickson · Founder and CEO

Mugonyi Edrine Dickson is the founder and CEO of CIN. He is an application developer and a student of Information Technology and Computing at Kyambogo University in Kampala, Uganda.

He designed, built and runs CIN, bringing together his interest in application development and his ambition to create digital experiences that help people express themselves and connect. His work on CIN is part of a wider commitment to learning, building and contributing to technology from Uganda.

What guides our work

Values we want CIN to stand for.

These principles describe the direction we aim to follow as the platform grows.

Creativity

Make room for original ideas, personal expression and the courage to create.

Connection

Help people discover one another and build communities around shared interests.

Inspiration

Encourage people to learn from others, share what they know and keep creating.

Respect

Promote thoughtful participation and a community where people treat one another with dignity.

Keep learning, listening and improving the product as CIN develops.

Communicate honestly about the platform, its capabilities and its development.
