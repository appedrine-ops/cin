CIN - Create. Inspire. Network.

CIN is a free short-video, photo and stories app built in Uganda. People share what they create, follow each other, chat, and join circles around the things they love.

Open the CIN app

Founder and CEO

Mugonyi Edrine Dickson is the founder and CEO of CIN. He is an application developer and a student of Information Technology and Computing at Kyambogo University in Kampala, Uganda. He designed, built and runs CIN.

What CIN offers

  • Short videos, photos and 24-hour stories
  • Verified accounts with colored ticks
  • Direct messages, circles and live streaming
  • Creator analytics, hashtags and sounds
  • Free to use, installable on your phone
